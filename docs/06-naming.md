# Naming

## Criteria

Short · easy to pronounce · easy to spell after hearing it once · modern ·
brandable · available as app name, domain, and social handle. One word, or two
very short words. Doesn't need "push-up" in it.

The one-pager's real ambition is that the brand becomes synonymous with the
activity. That argues for a name that **works as a verb**: *"Did you press
today?"* reads like a habit. *"Did you use PushTrack Pro today?"* does not.

## Candidates

| Name | Read | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Press** | A push-up *is* a press. Clean, confident, verbs naturally. | Strongest verb test of the set. Common word, so the App Store name and domain will be contested — likely needs a modifier domain (`press.fit`, `getpress.app`). |
| **Piston** | Mechanical up-down imagery, energetic, memorable. | Distinctive enough to actually own. Slightly harder to verb. |
| **Hundo** | Playful, nods at 100 push-ups, very modern. | Matches "slightly playful" well. Risks feeling tied to one milestone. |
| **Cadence** | Rhythm, tempo, consistency — the habit, not the exercise. | Elegant but abstract, and used elsewhere in fitness/cycling. |
| **Hup** | Two letters from a count-off. Short, punchy, verbs perfectly. | Almost certainly available. Risks reading slightly military — check against the "not military" constraint. |
| **Onward** | Warm, motivating, about progress. | Longer, less punchy, weaker verb. |
| **Vert** | The vertical travel of every rep. Modern, short. | Strongly associated with skateboarding and jump-height apps. |
| **Tally** | The counting itself. Friendly, unpretentious. | Generic; hard to own, easy to confuse with note-taking apps. |
| **Daily Push** | Says exactly what it is; zero explanation needed. | Descriptive rather than brandable, and it's already a program name inside the app. A good fallback, not a great brand. |

## Recommendation

Check availability on **Press**, **Piston**, and **Hup** first — they're the
three that pass the verb test and fit the visual direction without reading as
either military or cartoonish.

A practical note on process: check the App Store name, the `.com`/`.app`
domain, and the Instagram/TikTok handle *together* before falling in love with
one. Also run a quick USPTO search in class 9 (software) and 41 (fitness
services) — a name that clears the App Store but collides with a registered
fitness mark is a rebrand waiting to happen.

**Availability above is unverified** — none of these have been checked against
the App Store, domain registrars, social handles, or trademark databases. Treat
the list as directions to explore, not as cleared options.
