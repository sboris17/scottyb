# Scripts

Produced with the prompt in [`../prompts/script.md`](../prompts/script.md).

Six scripts, one niche: the economics of being a creator. They're written to work
alone but to compound in order — batch 1 sets up the problems, batch 2 explains
why the problems exist and what an actual asset looks like. Three of them
independently arrive at the same conclusion from different directions, which is
the spine of the series.

## Batch 1 — the surface

| Script | Thesis |
| --- | --- |
| [`sidemen-group-moat.md`](sidemen-group-moat.md) | The group is a structural moat a solo creator can't build at any budget. |
| [`the-access-business.md`](the-access-business.md) | Audiences buy access to a person, and access can't be scaled. |
| [`the-click-ate-the-content.md`](the-click-ate-the-content.md) | The thumbnail isn't packaging, it's the first sentence of the video. |

## Batch 2 — the machinery

| Script | Thesis |
| --- | --- |
| [`your-niche-is-your-salary.md`](your-niche-is-your-salary.md) | You're paid for the commercial value of your audience, not for views. |
| [`you-dont-own-them.md`](you-dont-own-them.md) | The subscribe button is a preference signal. Everyone is a tenant. |
| [`the-back-catalogue-doesnt-pay.md`](the-back-catalogue-doesnt-pay.md) | The archive doesn't earn, so a decade of work builds a job, not capital. |

## Publish order

1. `sidemen-group-moat` — the recognisable name does the discovery work.
2. `your-niche-is-your-salary` — the most immediately useful, best retention.
3. `the-click-ate-the-content` — fully evergreen, no names or dates to decay.
4. `the-access-business` — the deeper cut for people who came back.
5. `you-dont-own-them` — needs #1 already up to link to, or it ends on a
   diagnosis with no treatment.
6. `the-back-catalogue-doesnt-pay` — last, because it makes the other five look
   like they were building toward something.

## What's in each file

Narration of roughly 1,450–1,500 words (~10 minutes at 150 wpm), a loop map with
timestamps, eight ranked title options, a thumbnail direction, and a **Before you
record** section listing every claim to verify.

## House rules

- **No invented specifics.** No numbers, dates, counts, RPMs, or valuation
  multiples unless verified from a primary source. This niche fact-checks in the
  comments, and being confidently wrong once costs more than the video earned.
- **No named individuals as cautionary tales.** Structural arguments don't need a
  villain, and a real person's burnout, ban, or bad year is not b-roll.
- **Arguments stay labelled as arguments.** Where a script is reasoning rather
  than reporting, the notes say so, and the narration is phrased so it reads that
  way out loud.
- **Narration only in the script body.** No shot lists or stage directions; that
  lives in the notes underneath.
